# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment free environment for everyone who participates in this project.

Participation includes contributing code, reporting issues, reviewing pull requests, discussing ideas, and interacting in other project spaces.

## Expected Behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Welcome different perspectives and levels of experience.
- Give feedback about the work rather than attacking the person.
- Accept reasonable feedback without hostility.
- Respect personal boundaries and privacy.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Help maintain an inclusive and professional environment.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or discrimination.
- Personal insults, hostile comments, or repeated disruptive behaviour.
- Sexualised language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate misinformation, spam, or malicious contributions.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make another participant feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and whenever someone is representing the project publicly.

Project spaces include issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, commits, and other repository interactions.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed in `SECURITY.md`.

Include enough information to understand what happened, such as links, screenshots, dates, and relevant context.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary.

Enforcement decisions will consider the seriousness, frequency, and impact of the behaviour.

## Good-Faith Participation

Disagreement is allowed. Harassment is not.

Mistakes can be corrected. Repeated or deliberate misconduct may lead to removal from the project.
